# FAQ-COM-010 · Are your products Matter and Thread compatible?

> Revision draft pending review. The original website answer is quoted below; replacing it requires approval and a separate publication step.

## Original website answer (current, for comparison only)

Yes — Rez-TI is positioned as China's first pure Matter-native smart home solution provider, with Thread support across our connected product lines.

## Answer (proposed)

Compatibility must be confirmed for the specific model, specification and control version. In the custom lighting collection, non-smart three-colour switching, Xiaoai and Matter over Thread are separate options; an available Matter configuration does not mean every product or configuration supports it, and other product lines must be checked individually too. Where the same item is offered in both Matter over Wi-Fi and Matter over Thread, the choice depends on site conditions: stable Wi-Fi with few nodes favours Wi-Fi, while high device density or a need for low power and local response favours Thread, which requires at least one compatible Thread border router on site; the two versions can be mixed within one ecosystem. Send the product link and intended platform so we can confirm a suitable configuration.

## Scope

A proposed replacement for the broad wording in website FAQ item 10. Individual capabilities remain subject to the selected SKU's documentation and confirmation.

## Basis and open items (internal verification, not published with the answer)

- Basis: the original website answer above and [Sources](../sources/README.md).
- This change: keeps the original "confirm per model" conclusion and adds how to choose between the Wi-Fi and Thread versions, including the border-router requirement, so the answer no longer implies hub-free operation.
- Removed: the unverified claims about being the first provider in China and Thread support across all connected product lines.
- Open: the protocol-version coverage list per model.
