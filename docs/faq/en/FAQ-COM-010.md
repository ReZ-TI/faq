# FAQ-COM-010 · Are all your products compatible with Matter and Thread?

> Draft for review. Not published to the website.

## Answer

Compatibility must be confirmed for the model, specification, and control version. In the custom lighting collection, non-smart three-color switching, Xiaoai, and Matter over Thread are separate options. An available Matter configuration does not mean every product or configuration supports it. Other product lines must also be checked individually. Send the product link and your intended platform so we can confirm a suitable configuration.

## Scope

A proposed replacement for the broad wording in the existing website FAQ item 10. Individual capabilities remain subject to the selected SKU's documentation and confirmation.

## Internal verification notes (not for public publication)

The existing claims about being the first provider in China and all connected products supporting Thread were not verified in this review. Removing those general claims does not assert that any specific existing device is incompatible.
