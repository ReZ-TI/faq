# FAQ-COM-019 · Do you support prototyping and small-batch pilot production? How is NRE priced?

> Draft pending review. Not published to the website.

## Answer

Yes. We can take on prototyping and small-batch pilot runs, and support feasibility assessment, mechanical and electrical recommendations and design-for-manufacture feedback. Engineering fees (NRE) typically start at USD 3,000; the final figure is assessed separately against product complexity, the engineering scope we take on and the materials involved, and lead time varies with scope. Please send the requirement description, target quantity, timing and how far you want us to go: electronics only, module level, or complete unit. We will quote formally once we have the requirements.

For example, when adapting our existing Type 86 switch to a Type 120 switch for the US market without major changes to software functionality, we can complete the project's R&D and prototyping and provide the first 2–5 product prototypes for USD 3,000. Whether this example applies, and the specific engineering scope and deliverables, must be confirmed for each project.

## Scope

Assessed per project. Standard models already in mass production do not go through this route and follow the existing quotation process.

## Basis and open items (caliber notes, not published with the answer)

- Where the question comes from: a question repeated in recent customer enquiries.
- Caliber: accepting prototyping and pilot production, NRE starting at USD 3,000, and the example above covering R&D, prototyping and the first 2–5 product prototypes when adapting a Type 86 switch to a Type 120 switch for the US market without major software changes are customer-facing positions confirmed by the business owner.
- Pending business confirmation: the engineering scope and number of prototypes for other projects, minimum pilot quantity, whether structural or certification work is included, and specific lead times must be confirmed per project. The example above is not extended into a standard package for all projects.
