# FAQ-COM-019 · Do you support prototyping and small-batch pilot production? How is NRE priced?

## Answer

Yes. We can take on prototyping and small-batch pilot runs, and support feasibility assessment, mechanical and electrical recommendations and design-for-manufacture feedback. For projects that require paid engineering work, engineering fees (NRE) typically start at USD 3,000; the final figure is assessed separately against product complexity, the engineering scope we take on and the materials involved, and lead time varies with scope. Please send the requirement description, target quantity, timing and how far you want us to go: electronics only, module level, or complete unit. We will quote formally once we have the requirements.

For example, when adapting our existing Type 86 switch to a Type 120 switch for the US market without major changes to software functionality, we can complete the project's R&D and prototyping and provide the first 2–5 product prototypes for USD 3,000. We confirm whether this example applies, and the specific engineering scope and deliverables, for each project. It is not a standard package or a certification quote.

## Scope

Assessed per project. Standard models already in mass production do not go through this route and follow the existing quotation process.
