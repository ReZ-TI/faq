# FAQ-COM-018 · Do you accept regional distributors or resellers?

> Draft pending review. Not published to the website.

## Answer

Yes. We assess regional distribution, reseller and installation-service partnerships by country and product category. The scope can include equipment sales, installation and commissioning, after-sales and technical support. Whether a territory is exclusive, what the terms are and how dealer pricing is structured are confirmed in writing after assessing the target market, expected volume and existing channels. Please send your company profile, covered area, current customer base and first-year target so we can route you to the colleague responsible for that market.

## Scope

Whether we open a partnership and whether a territory can be granted exclusively are assessed case by case against existing channel arrangements, and confirmed in writing by both sides. This answer is not a territory commitment and contains no pricing terms.

## Basis and open items (caliber notes, not published with the answer)

- Where the question comes from: a question repeated in recent customer enquiries.
- Caliber: accepting regional partnerships is our customer-facing business position.
- Pending business confirmation: exclusivity conditions, dealer price structure, territory protection and volume commitments have no written policy; no price appears in the public answer.
