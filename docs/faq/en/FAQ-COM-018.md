# FAQ-COM-018 · Do you accept regional distributors or resellers?

## Answer

We consider regional distribution, reseller and installation-service partnerships by country and product category. A partnership can cover equipment sales, installation and commissioning, after-sales service and technical support. We assess whether to open a partnership or grant regional exclusivity case by case, taking existing channel arrangements into account. Any arrangement is subject to written confirmation by both parties. Please share your company profile, the area you cover, your current customer base and your first-year volume target so we can put you in touch with the right team.
