# FAQ-COM-011 · Which product categories do you manufacture?

> Draft pending review. Not published to the website.

## Answer

We cover smart switches and scene panels, mmWave presence sensors, curtain and roller-blind motors, VRF air-conditioner interfaces, 0/1–10V dimming modules, and Home Assistant hubs with Thread border routers. ESP32 modules and development kits are available per project. The exact model, control option and certification status have to be confirmed per SKU — send us the categories and target quantities and we will map them to models before quoting.

## Scope

The product lines listed above. It does not mean every model offers all protocols, features or certifications.
