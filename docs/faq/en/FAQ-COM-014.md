# FAQ-COM-014 · Do you support Matter certification transfer?

## Answer

Yes. We are a Participant member of the Connectivity Standards Alliance (CSA), and all our certified Matter products support the Certification Transfer Program (CTP).

Through CTP, a product can be re-certified under your own brand and sold under that brand once approved through the CSA process. Please contact our sales team with the target product, brand and planned changes. We will prepare a certification-transfer plan and confirm the process, cost and timing.

## Scope

The statement that all products support CTP refers to our certified Matter products. Transfers follow CSA CTP requirements: the certified end product is held and enrolled by a Participant or Promoter member, the applicant is eligible, and the product retains its certified functions, device type and capabilities. Uncertified new products, non-Matter items and custom functional changes require a separate assessment of the applicable process.
