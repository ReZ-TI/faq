# FAQ-COM-021 · Can you handle complete electromechanical assembly, or only certain parts?

## Answer

Both are open for discussion. We can supply only the module and electronics, or take on complete electromechanical assembly including structural parts, wiring, assembly, testing and packaging. How far we can go depends on the product structure and our current line capability, and is confirmed after reviewing drawings or a sample. Please state how far you want us to go: PCBA only, module level, or complete unit shipment.

## Scope

Assessed per project and model; this does not mean every category can be fully assembled by us.
