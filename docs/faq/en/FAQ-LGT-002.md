# FAQ-LGT-002 · What should I provide for a custom lighting quotation, and who will quote?

> Draft for review. Not published to the website.

## Answer

Send the product link or model, size or number of heads, color, control option, quantity, and delivery location. The website supports product discovery and inquiries. Wenzhou ReZ-TI Smart Home Co., Ltd. will confirm your requirements and issue a formal quotation. Final configuration, taxes, shipping, and delivery terms are subject to the agreed quotation and order.

## Scope

Website inquiries for this custom lighting collection. For overseas projects, also see FAQ-INT-001.

## Internal verification notes (not for public publication)

This answer does not promise a standard lead time, stock availability, or shipping terms. These must be confirmed for the inquiry.
