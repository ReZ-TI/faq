# FAQ-COM-020 · How do you handle NDAs, IP and tooling ownership?

> Draft pending review. Not published to the website.

## Answer

We can sign an NDA. Each side retains the intellectual property it already owned; ownership and usage rights of results newly created in the project are agreed in writing per project. Ownership, use and transfer conditions for tooling and fixtures are likewise agreed in writing per project. Final terms are set in the NDA and development agreement both sides sign.

## Scope

Agreed per project. This answer is not a standard clause and not a general commitment.

## Basis and open items (caliber notes, not published with the answer)

- Where the question comes from: a question repeated in recent customer enquiries.
- Caliber: signing NDAs and agreeing IP and tooling ownership per project is our customer-facing business position.
- Pending business confirmation: the standard NDA template and the specific terms for ownership, use and transfer of tooling and fixtures need legal and business confirmation.
