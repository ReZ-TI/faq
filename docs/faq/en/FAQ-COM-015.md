# FAQ-COM-015 · Can you provide CE and RoHS compliance documents?

> Revision draft. The website version from before this revision is retained below for comparison; website synchronization is recorded separately.

## Website version before this revision (for comparison only)

We provide the applicable compliance documents per model, including test reports and declarations of conformity. Requirements differ between markets — the EU, Israel and the Middle East each ask for a different set — so tell us the target model and destination country and we will check the documents that apply to that model and send them together. If no such documents exist for that model, we will say so directly rather than offer a substitute assurance.

## Answer (proposed)

We can provide existing test reports, declarations of conformity and other applicable documents for each product model.

As standards and certification requirements vary between countries and regions, we cannot guarantee that every product already meets all the requirements of your target market. Please tell us which product models you are interested in and the countries or regions where you plan to sell them, and we will check the existing certification and compliance documents for you.

If a product has not yet obtained the certification required in your target market but you are interested in our products, we warmly invite you to explore becoming a local distributor, jointly assess market demand and work together on the relevant certification process. The specific certification plan, costs and cooperation arrangements can be discussed further for each project.

## Scope

Existing certification and compliance documents are checked by product model and intended country or region of sale. Where further certification is needed, the specific plan, costs and cooperation arrangements are discussed per project.

## Basis and open items (caliber notes, not published with the answer)

- Where the question comes from: a question repeated in recent customer enquiries.
- This change: clarify that existing documents are provided per model without guaranteeing that every product already meets all target-market requirements. For products that have not yet obtained the required certification, invite discussion of local distribution, jointly assess demand and cooperate on the certification process, with the specific plan, costs and cooperation arrangements discussed per project.
- Pending business confirmation: the specific certification plan, costs and cooperation arrangements for each target market. The public answer lists no standard numbers and promises no certificate approval.
