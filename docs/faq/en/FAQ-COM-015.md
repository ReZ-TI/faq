# FAQ-COM-015 · Can you provide CE and RoHS compliance documents?

> Draft pending review. Not published to the website.

## Answer

We provide the applicable compliance documents per model, including test reports and declarations of conformity. Requirements differ between markets — the EU, Israel and the Middle East each ask for a different set — so tell us the target model and destination country and we will check the documents that apply to that model and send them together. If no such documents exist for that model, we will say so directly rather than offer a substitute assurance.

## Scope

Models tested against the relevant standards. The exact standard coverage depends on the actual report for that model and does not extend to untested models.

- Where the question comes from: a question repeated in recent customer enquiries.
