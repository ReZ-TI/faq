# FAQ-COM-015 · Can you provide CE and RoHS compliance documents?

> Draft pending review. Not published to the website.

## Answer

We provide the applicable compliance documents per model, including test reports and declarations of conformity. Requirements differ between markets — the EU, Israel and the Middle East each ask for a different set — so tell us the target model and destination country and we will check the documents that apply to that model and send them together.

## Scope

Models tested against the relevant standards. The exact standard coverage depends on the actual report for that model.

## Basis and open items (internal verification, not published with the answer)

- Basis: [Sources and confirmed business rules](../sources/README.md).
- Open: laboratory name, standard coverage and validity period of existing reports are not consolidated; whether reports can be issued under a customer's brand name is undecided. The public answer lists no standard numbers and promises no laboratory accreditation.
