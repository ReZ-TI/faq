# FAQ-COM-015 · Can you provide CE and RoHS compliance documents?

> Revision draft pending review. The current website version is quoted below; replacing it requires approval and a separate publication step.

## Current website version (for comparison only)

We provide the applicable compliance documents per model, including test reports and declarations of conformity. Requirements differ between markets — the EU, Israel and the Middle East each ask for a different set — so tell us the target model and destination country and we will check the documents that apply to that model and send them together. If no such documents exist for that model, we will say so directly rather than offer a substitute assurance.

## Answer (proposed)

We provide the applicable compliance documents per model, including test reports and declarations of conformity. Requirements differ between markets: the EU works on the CE framework, while Israel, the Middle East and South America (Brazil, for example) each have their own rules, and some countries also require local certification or registration involving the importer or a local representative. Tell us the target model and destination country and we will check which documents apply to that model and whether local certification is needed, then send them together. If no such documents exist for that model, we will say so directly rather than offer a substitute assurance.

## Scope

Models tested against the relevant standards. Exact standard coverage and local certification requirements depend on the actual report for that model and on the regulations of the target countries and regions of sale or deployment, and do not extend to untested models.

## Basis and open items (caliber notes, not published with the answer)

- Where the question comes from: a question repeated in recent customer enquiries.
- This change: the current version lists only the EU, Israel and the Middle East. It now adds South America (Brazil) as a distinct case and states that some countries require local certification with importer involvement, so "documents per model" is not presented as "market access already granted".
- Pending business confirmation: the specific market-access route for Brazil and similar markets, which side carries certification and registration, and whether documents can be issued under a customer's brand. The public answer lists no standard numbers and promises no certificate approval.
