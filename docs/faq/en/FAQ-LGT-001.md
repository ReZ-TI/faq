# FAQ-LGT-001 · Which control options are available for custom lighting?

> Draft for review. Not published to the website.

## Answer

Depending on the model and specification, this lighting collection offers non-smart three-color switching, Xiaoai, or Matter over Thread options. Select from the combinations available for the specific product. Include your preferred control option in the inquiry so Wenzhou ReZ-TI can confirm the configuration and quotation.

## Scope

The custom lighting collection covered by the confirmed catalog rules. Matter availability follows the eligible LED fixture scope and does not apply automatically to every product.

## Internal verification notes (not for public publication)

The driver is adapted before shipment. Electrical specifications, ecosystem features, and certifications require confirmation for the selected configuration.
