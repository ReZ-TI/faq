# FAQ-LGT-003 · Does the Matter over Thread option control both the light and the fan?

> Draft for review. Not published to the website.

## Answer

For the fan lights in this custom lighting collection, the Matter over Thread option controls the light only. It does not include control of the fan motor. If you also need smart fan-speed or motor control, state that separately in your inquiry so we can confirm an available solution.

## Scope

Fan lights offering the Matter option in this catalog. This statement does not describe other unverified fan products.
